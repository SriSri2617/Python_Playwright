3 Practice user stories

User Story 1 : As a user, I want to view the Agile Helper page, 
so that I can access information about Agile and Scrum practices.

Scenario:
    - Navigate to https://lejonmanen.github.io/agile-helper/
    - Verify that the Agile Helper page loads successfully
    - Verify that the page title "Agile Helper" is displayed
    - Verify that Swedish and British flag icon is displayed
    - Verify that "Vilken dag under sprinten är det?" heading is displayed
    - Verify that the buttons "Första", "Någonstans mitt i", and "Sista" are visible
    - Verify that the user can interact with the page

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

User Story 2 : As a user, I want to view the Agile Helper page in English, 
so that I can understand the information even if I do not speak Swedish.

Scenario:
    - Navigate to https://lejonmanen.github.io/agile-helper/
    - Verify that the British flag icon is displayed
    - Click the British flag icon
    - Verify that the page content is translated to English
    - Verify that the buttons are displayed in English
    - Verify that the user can still access the sprint information and meeting instructions in English
    
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

User Story 3 : As a user, I want to see instructions for Sprint Planning on the first day of the sprint, 
so that I know how to prepare the sprint backlog.    

Scenario: 
    - Navigate to https://lejonmanen.github.io/agile-helper/
    - Click the "First" button
    - Verify that "Start off the sprint with Sprint planning" and "Start every day with Daily standup" buttons displayed
    - Click the "Sprint planning" button
    - Verify that a dialog appears
    - Verify that the dialog contains the heading "Sprint planning"
    - Verify that instructions for planning the sprint are displayed
    - Click on "Ok we're done.Start the sprint" button
    - Verify that the dialog disappears
    - Verify that user remains on the "First day of sprint." page
     
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

User Story 4 : As a user, I want to see instructions for Daily Standup, 
so that I know what to discuss during the daily meeting.

Scenario: 
    - Navigate to https://lejonmanen.github.io/agile-helper/
    - Click the "First" button
    - Verify that "Start off the sprint with Sprint planning" is displayed
     - Verify that "Start every day with Daily standup" button is displayed

    - Verify that the dialog contains the heading "Daily standup"
    - Verify that instructions for standup meeting are displayed
    - Click the "Start the meeting: 10 minutes" button
    - Verify that "Time left" counter starts counting down
    - Click on "Ok we're done!"

User Story 5 : As a user, I want to choose another day in the sprint, 
so that I can view the activities and meetings that take place on different days of the sprint.

Scenario:
    - Navigate to https://lejonmanen.github.io/agile-helper/
    - Click the "First" button
    - Verify that the "First day of the sprint" view is displayed
    - Click the "Choose another day" button
    - Verify that the day selection options are displayed
    - Verify that the "First", "Somewhere in the middle", and "Last" buttons are displayed
    - Click the "Somewhere in the middle" button
    - Verify that the "Any day during the sprint" view is displayed
    - Click the "Choose another day" button
    - Click the "Last" button
    - Verify that the " Final day of sprint" page is displayed
